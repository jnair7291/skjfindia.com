About the organisation

The federation

A national community committed to disciplined Shotokan training, instructor development, and meaningful opportunities for karateka.

Our story

Rooted in tradition.
Growing across India.

Established in 2004, Shotokan Karatedo Japan Federation - India promotes karate as a lifelong practice of discipline, physical ability, self-control, and character.

Through regular instruction, seminars, grading, and competition, SKJF India gives students and instructors a shared framework in which to learn, test their ability, and progress. The federation connects dojo leaders across several Indian states while maintaining a clear link to the wider Shotokan community.
