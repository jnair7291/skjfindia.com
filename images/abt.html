Soke Masahiro Motomura

WBKL President Black Belt 10th Dan

Nihon Shotokan Karatedo - Japan

In 1981, established WBKL aime to creation of 21st century Karate by the exchange full contact Karate and traditional Karate. At that time, traditional Karate and kyokushin conflicted intensely. However we were convinced that the new age Karate will create by notconfrontation but exchange. It is proved correct by this 30 years. Now the WBKL championship is a authority in this field in Japan. I think participation in different rule game is a serious adventure .But the person who learned other style karate became a final winner.Karate is martial arts. The martial artist cannot say the strong man even if he wins in his rule.The martial artist can say the strong man by fighting in enemy's rule and winning. It was at the 1960's that Muay Thai landed in Japan. A lot of Karate-ka fought in the rule of Muay Thai and he was defeated. However, it would fight in the rule of Muay Thai before long, it won, and the person who proved strength of Karate showed up. Karae faced a big technical improvement by them. We see one of the utopias of Karate-Ka them. It would win in the rule of Muay Thai before long, and the person who proved strength of Karate appeared.


Karae faced a big technical improvement by them. We see one of the utopias of Karate-Ka them.Such thought was accepted early in foreign countries. And it is a populer method now. Recently this method has come to be shown in Japan. I feel deep pleasure as a pioneer.


The Shihans of WBKL are from various style, Shotokan-ryu, Goju-ryu, Shito-ryu, JKF, JKA, Okinawa karate,Ju-jutsu, kyokushin.And we are sharing the knowleadge and tecnique each other. It is a strong point of our WBKL .

 

 

 

 

 

Saiko Shihan Babu S.Nair

 

Saiko Shihan Babu Nair has Started Karate Training Since 1976. He has Experience of More than 37 Years of Training in Karate and Teaching professionally for more than 30 years, and have trained thousands of students, many of whom have Won Several Medals in State, National as well as International Championship.


Achieved the title "SAIKO SHIHAN" from GRAND MASTER MASAHIRO MOTOMURA from JAPAN in year 2011. Saiko Shihan Babu S.Nair has won many competitions and is now producing his own humble champions.He has been visiting Japan twice in a year and participate in their tournaments as well as attends the training programs.


Established SHOTOKAN KARATEDO JAPAN FURINKAZAN - SOUTH ASIA and SHOTOKAN KARATEDO JAPAN FEDERATION - INDIA in year 2008. Organizes SOUTH ASIA level tournament every year, open to all to participate and spread this art in true spirit.
